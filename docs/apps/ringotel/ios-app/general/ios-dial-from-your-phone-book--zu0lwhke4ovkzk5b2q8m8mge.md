To call a phone number that is in your device’s contacts list and is not imported into the mobile app:

1. Open the ‘**Menu**’ screen and select the ‘**Phone Contacts**’ menu item.

![](/img/ringotel/original/c006e1dc80e9d268746e/214328149e11fe1dd8f9e0fdb9dedba2b897c2d0cc23077e00edd384739cb9d3.png)

1. On the screen with the list of your contacts, tap on a contact you want to call.
2. Press the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/9981afcb838199f838e3/456bc1831988045ace4340d6cb13ef670f2b907b8f41d9bc45b46a623455519e.png)icon to make a call.

![](/img/ringotel/original/15ef78caf15254f63934/22e13acddfb227bbcec691c28c4d188c0626cc33c84ad748768e99cfd85e31a3.png)

**Alternatively**

1. Open the ‘**Keypad**’ tab
2. Press the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/2ed4425297b9673088b4/4c58022126ade37f7c2a26c377b2a59de746a1a5dd3fb90e86e7010ec4016ee5.png)icon on the bottom left corner of the numpad.

![](/img/ringotel/original/d950801451c68b52f0c5/cb8a1d79dcefc5e7530684524b99525276409959293807bff646df44daf1eae9.png)
