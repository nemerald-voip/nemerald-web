*Looking for an&#x20;*[PC/Mac](/docs/apps/desktop-call-parking--plo8imk946vya1f7dapzaatq) *or*[*Android*](/docs/apps/android-call-parking--vki9zw9odl9s6mdnxqnhi7vy)*version of this article?*

Call Parking feature allows you to put a call on hold from one extension (or device) and retrieve it from another extension (or device) with permission to do so.

To park a call:

1. During an ongoing call, press the ‘**Transfer**’ icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/b427aeba262a7f68c9e5/d0e3ed4c40a8dd53aaf31cd446c20e5b6876e621b0629f5a2b498569901d8577.png)

![](/img/ringotel/original/b8e5c4b2ce737504717f/79a62a6e92c6a407b64224ba437630d05c31d018ff48bc9ef7b71ce6d8edc56b.png)

1. Select a parking slot where you would like to park a call and confirm action.

**Note**: if all parking slots are busy, you will not see any parking slots in the list.

![](/img/ringotel/original/edb8bfc351b898686012/0d0fca22298c3f4d424c3c6ff35f7c5841005312008f283c4d7a7ba75651b22e.png)

## Retrieve a parked call

A parked call will be displayed in the Contacts tab above the list of users/contacts. It will be visible to all your colleagues, and they will be able to pick up this call from a parking slot.

1. Navigate to the Contacts tab and click on a parking slot to retrieve a call.
2. Confirm action in the pop-up window to connect with the call waiting on a parking slot.

*\* Please check the availability of this feature with your PBX Administrator.*
