When you make dozens of calls every day, it is tough to remember the details of every single call. By filling in a call subject and adding some comments, you can highlight the most important parts of your conversation and easily find them when you need them later.

**To view/add information about a call**:

1. Open your conversation history with a contact. You can do that by switching to the ‘**Calls**’ tab and selecting a contact from the list of your calls.

![](/img/ringotel/original/5bb53ceb2a45982abb12/224d54ad1897795fe40d340a373d7028a9cbbe23c3af1d8495733a0b367bec96.png)

1. After selecting a contact, you’ll see a history of all communication with that contact. Tap on a call you’d like to add details to.

![](/img/ringotel/original/bda68733d21cea32734b/0e421d0d34ed5e86487c2f8a104187a0ec6c9fecbcedcf699485ec51dd7e9427.png)

![](/img/ringotel/original/d5955555481574ace3db/b46331d7064b29f776e938952d2feac4d5e8d486fd1096def11e9e2ba56f199b.png)

## Finding a call by its subject

Specifying the subject of a call can help you easily find that call any time later.

To find a call by its subject, simply type a subject you want to find in the Chat Search field and press the ‘**Search**’ button.

![](/img/ringotel/original/e43f70067aabf6319d53/d0ef328db4c61f264c2b18c0e20af48e4f3d061242187e33bb7a3483fb2d5c60.png)

You can also mark a call as a ‘Favorite’ by using the long tap on a message or a call. This will make it easier to find it using chat filters.
