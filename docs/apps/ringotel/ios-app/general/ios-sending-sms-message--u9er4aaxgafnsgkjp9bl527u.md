*Looking for an&#x20;*[Android](/docs/apps/android-sending-messages--ztffi0qzjlvj88mlxkg556r3) *or&#x20;*[PC/Mac](/docs/apps/desktop-creating-chat-sms--wq59474ron4rofgdi73acjxw) *version of this article?*

To send an SMS message to a contact or a phone number:

1. Press on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/c4c15a8e5eb3ee1b8b03/fa3a72562096b836374e36cd269b0df33a7f0e2f9e4e5848d2541289d92ba5fc.png)icon at the top right corner of the screen and then tap on the SMS menu item to create a new chat.
2. Select a contact you’d like to send an SMS message to or type in a phone number at the top of the screen.

![](/img/ringotel/original/d4c4deebebd9aed0a13c/50a8eb5f9fabbf7b1dc535db562e70b1f55f8a3601a1fd4b90e0af82a3d93ace.png)

1. Tap on the text area at the bottom of the screen and type in your message.

![](/img/ringotel/original/a1abfc9e08dd98fbd873/e05c900396e5f7830b9967fe8682cd1aaf8cc38c5ba61f468deda547dfcf9540.png)

1. After you’ve finished typing your message, press the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/1ecb72eec15ddb023269/8efc7d22da2c89d092dffcd1ec89f74b4e170f32bce9e35b62ba73645c18591d.png)icon to send it.

*\* Please check the availability of this feature with your PBX Administrator.*

## Sending voice messages

To send a voice message to your colleagues who are using the app:

1. Open a chat with a colleague(s) you would like to send a file to.
2. Tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/9f4af85ac9f03d3fb207/497219ab780d5454f401edc0ecb7cf746ac096cf7d7d63abb5f9bd31071beddc.png)icon at the bottom of the screen.
3. The recording screen will pop up where you’ll be able to pause/resume and reset your voice recording.

![](/img/ringotel/original/480aaacfdc63cbd49c11/5b79a9a609178ec85b006888f3626aa119a6951b865bdad3af2f79d6e441a0c0.png)
