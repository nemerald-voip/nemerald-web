To import a contact from your iOS device into the mobile app:

1. Open the ‘**Contacts**’ tab and tap on the '**Phone Contacts**'.

![](/img/ringotel/original/d52214e7a3e2f2fedaf8/c811081839173a132bc651fa7f70cee1efaf47b0f41690d6a9528e7403887235.png)

1. Tap on the ‘**Import ’**&#x62;utton to select the contacts you want to import.

![](/img/ringotel/original/3cf01f762067c48aefc7/fea34d220ddd14c96fbe241479a885c4762231b678f0d930933b51f0f00b4a2e.png)

1. Select the contacts you would like to import and press the ‘**Done**’ button to finish importing.

![](/img/ringotel/original/db1548e09d8a22c5742b/36d796eed378245f31c7a720093fa6bbd0e7c4af509115ddf662c5258fc36401.png)
