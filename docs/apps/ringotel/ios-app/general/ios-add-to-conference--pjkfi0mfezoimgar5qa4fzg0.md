*Looking for an&#x20;*[PC/Mac](/docs/apps/desktop-conference-calls--vaqva2dc377xnjga0me78u8p) *or&#x20;*[Android](/docs/apps/android-add-participant--revxed1mtc961qnyoj91m8rz) *version of this article?*

To add a participant to a call:

1. During an ongoing call, tap on the ‘**Add call**’ icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/6b5fffabba5d60506ca5/84a30dfd13ed069f173ad424c7317fc2ee782d024917d073fffb809b12881587.png)

![](/img/ringotel/original/b8e5c4b2ce737504717f/79a62a6e92c6a407b64224ba437630d05c31d018ff48bc9ef7b71ce6d8edc56b.png)

1. Choose а contact that you’d like to add to a call from the list.

**OR&#x20;**&#x70;ress the numpad icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/140fae854ca2089ce82e/27582fef43b624850b4b0519a90cc9506c69defb77026fca0c42b503969093d2.png)to dial a phone number.

![](/img/ringotel/original/86a79526278cac29ee54/82bbd4d24e0cb74bb3a2b2dba8003a147bf5ad8e25d1f817cc650bf85fcd3d11.png)

1. After you’ve connected with a new participant, press the ‘**Join**’ button to this participant to a conference.

![](/img/ringotel/original/fe68e0fc1c26269d0b1a/7e1b743528caf0684f70032a78dd2526e6fffdc211cecb3e88c87f9336fddb2d.png)

1. Press the ‘**Merge**’ button (1) to merge both lines into a conference.

You can repeat all the above steps to add more people to a conference call.
