## Copying a text message

To copy to a specific messagein a chat:

1. Long-tap on a message that you’d like to copy.

![](/img/ringotel/original/bda68733d21cea32734b/0e421d0d34ed5e86487c2f8a104187a0ec6c9fecbcedcf699485ec51dd7e9427.png)

1. In the pop-up menu, select the ‘Copy’ menu item.

![](/img/ringotel/original/9ef0d4ec0f30f71bce53/43945c99dbbba2c97db046c59bef9b7494f73ce477c48756f00e97d0d43385de.png)

## Forwarding messages

To forward **a single message&#x20;**&#x74;o another user(s):

1. Long-tap on a message that you’d like to transfer.
2. In the pop-up menu, select the ‘**Forward**’ menu item.

![](/img/ringotel/original/3c699c2f13887d6cd8f2/c10dec53de25ca40fa7ab7fdb4962c3636b09ff62fc4eae19e7a5a466813ad19.png)

1. Select contacts or group chats to which you would like to transfer a message.

![](/img/ringotel/original/f194ce9a88026f49ed04/1d3a97de97944a101f59b9279fe52a1552faa4e4100eca7b0bd54945f9c514f4.png)

To forward **multiple messages&#x20;**&#x74;o another user(s):

1. Long-tap on a message that you’d like to forward.
2. In the pop-up menu, select the ‘**More**’ menu item.

![](/img/ringotel/original/ef2d54249375999dbdde/148ec61c170be920c87d1701d41d8f9aa3ec4437753574a9bf994e6de1da1560.png)

1. Tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/9c141067cebceec0506f/7c211919e85df12944e5a188cff7017ff4ec89ecb3bd934376113b42f4899abb.png)icon.

![](/img/ringotel/original/945a313a3c54b9052681/e9aa4158948b866bfbc3b4c95e0e3070bc4bed152d7c40d6eeba1b3d88c6bf72.png)

1. Select contacts or group chats to which you would like to transfer a message.

![](/img/ringotel/original/f194ce9a88026f49ed04/1d3a97de97944a101f59b9279fe52a1552faa4e4100eca7b0bd54945f9c514f4.png)

## Replying to a message

To reply to a specific message in a chat:

1. Long-tap on a message that you’d like to reply to.
2. In the pop-up menu, select the ‘**Reply**’ menu item.

![](/img/ringotel/original/3c699c2f13887d6cd8f2/c10dec53de25ca40fa7ab7fdb4962c3636b09ff62fc4eae19e7a5a466813ad19.png)

1. At the bottom of the screen, right above the Text Area where you type your messages, you’ll see the message that you’re replying to.

![](/img/ringotel/original/8548f7ce9e5750b6a946/6ed6336df2c786b72a543ad3e8cd9f821e7758c4d8240d46c4ee4760c27032fb.png)

1. Type your reply in the text area at the bottom of the screen.
2. Press the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/1ecb72eec15ddb023269/8efc7d22da2c89d092dffcd1ec89f74b4e170f32bce9e35b62ba73645c18591d.png)icon to send your reply.

## Share messages with other applications on your device

To share a message with other applications:

1. Long-tap on any message in a chat.
2. In the pop-up menu, select the ‘**More**’ menu item.

![](/img/ringotel/original/ef2d54249375999dbdde/148ec61c170be920c87d1701d41d8f9aa3ec4437753574a9bf994e6de1da1560.png)

1. Select messages /photos /files /calls you would like to share.

![](/img/ringotel/original/945a313a3c54b9052681/e9aa4158948b866bfbc3b4c95e0e3070bc4bed152d7c40d6eeba1b3d88c6bf72.png)

1. Tap on an![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/2f46d45c9eb602ac80cc/ee3c37a631a13d89c2adedeafb23295a069ba1143eb685c7151a16a2679969d2.png)icon.
2. Select an application on your device you’d like to share messages with.
