*Looking for an&#x20;*[Android](/docs/apps/android-contacts--icbab0zkyvel86v5acnif2wn) *or&#x20;*[PC/Mac](/docs/apps/desktop-contacts-and-users-tabs--hrbdi37d949mb152d0llrw4p) *version of this article?*

The ‘Contacts’ Tab is where you can find contacts of all your colleagues and clients.

All your colleagues will be displayed in the ‘**My team**’ section, together with their presence statuses.

![](/img/ringotel/original/e989513bd91826402a65/e44c6cde24d96bb43ade7aa4de39d215ca8035acb89a2647572db7cf561d1e87.png)

1. Search for contacts.
2. Contacts added to Favorites.
3. List of your colleagues and external contacts.
