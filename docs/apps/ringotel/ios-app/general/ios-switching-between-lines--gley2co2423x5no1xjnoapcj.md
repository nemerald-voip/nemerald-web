During a call, if you need to make an outgoing call while putting the current call on hold:

1. Tap on the ‘**Add call**’ icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/6b5fffabba5d60506ca5/84a30dfd13ed069f173ad424c7317fc2ee782d024917d073fffb809b12881587.png)

![](/img/ringotel/original/b8e5c4b2ce737504717f/79a62a6e92c6a407b64224ba437630d05c31d018ff48bc9ef7b71ce6d8edc56b.png)

1. Choose а contact that you’d like to call from the list.

**OR&#x20;**&#x50;ress the Numpad icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/140fae854ca2089ce82e/27582fef43b624850b4b0519a90cc9506c69defb77026fca0c42b503969093d2.png)to dial a phone number.

![](/img/ringotel/original/86a79526278cac29ee54/82bbd4d24e0cb74bb3a2b2dba8003a147bf5ad8e25d1f817cc650bf85fcd3d11.png)

1. After the connection has been established, you can switch between the two calls by tapping on a pause icon.

![](/img/ringotel/original/5426e9575de7626bd66d/4447e97340c42b86adc432198a9b6267c1db0a6ac9aed7382c93120e4dfdc1a7.png)

1. Tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/781f587a10d66a302ddb/d32eb8e7539ce715ca240d4141a459407fe55a78bacc7b5ded9cf0aae028f5c6.png)icon to switch between lines.
