Upon receiving an incoming call, on your mobile device, you will see the following screen:

![](/img/ringotel/original/c70c9c2a959cfcfd63df/f2b59b71a3ad200c279653aab12f73ecb279c8b0e7da57351f77aa3cef2186f1.png)

1. Drop a call
2. Answer an incoming call

During a call, you have access to the following features:

![](/img/ringotel/original/8001350515bfb43c6ac1/370456b7f81981498cab84f2910539b684327d1d440bc7b757a49cc62365b9aa.png)

1. Open Contact’s Profile.
2. Put the call on hold.
3. Make a parallel call.
4. Mute / unmute microphone.
5. Turn the video on / off.
6. Turn call recording on / off.
7. Open the numpad to send DTMF.
8. Transfer a call.
9. Turn the speaker on / off.
10. Open a chat with a caller.
