You can block incoming calls and SMS from the selected contacts and phone numbers.

**NOTE:**&#x42;locked contacts are shared across your entire organisation, so if you block a number, it will also appear on other users' blocked list.

## How to block a new contact

To block a new number:

1. Navigate to Menu > Blocked.
2. Tap the "+" (plus) button.
3. In the appeared pop-up, specify the phone number and (optionally) the reason for blocking this number.
4. Press the Block button to confirm.

![](/img/ringotel/original/6846927446b7c10ae10b/fe94b54796a0a1c1c68c7391480a786adac5293013c69053be04fead18bac9da.png)

## How to block an existing contact

To block an existing contact:

1. Navigate to Contacts tab.
2. Find and select the contact in the list.
3. In the contact profile, scroll down and press the "Block" button.
4. In the appeared pop-up, (optionally) specify the reason for blocking this number.
5. Press the Block button to confirm.

![](/img/ringotel/original/ca3bb29fb1b1900e1668/8599b27f6e174eb810ac14adf9fdd8337450362a9d4adb3c2bd69a252019016a.png)

## How to unblock a contact

To unblock a blocked contact:

1. Navigate to Menu > Blocked.
2. Find and select the contact in the list.
3. In the blocked contact profile, scroll down and press the "Unblock" button.

![](/img/ringotel/original/7be927d9d3c7523298f1/4abbd4cef10172163682b5a9c3a78c1bd4c670bd14f78edea3553113fd149085.png)
