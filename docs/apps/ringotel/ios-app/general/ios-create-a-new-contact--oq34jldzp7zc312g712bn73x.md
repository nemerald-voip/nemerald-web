To create a new contact:

1. Tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/c4c15a8e5eb3ee1b8b03/fa3a72562096b836374e36cd269b0df33a7f0e2f9e4e5848d2541289d92ba5fc.png)icon at the top right corner of the screen.
2. Select ‘**Contact**’ from a pop-up menu:

![](/img/ringotel/original/4c0e43c36eb56bc70068/a29a523f960b4f242bb1d6f24375ee2fbc4cb5fd836bc23886e41b9a033243d1.png)

1. Then fill in the name of the contact and add additional details.

![](/img/ringotel/original/c449d75fd1003d633076/3783c5d06eec87b276688848dfde3e54c2c5d0e6f169199c641cc385dacfc667.png)

1. Enter a contact’s name
2. Press ‘Save’ to finish editing the contact.
3. Add an avatar picture to the contact.
4. Add details like phone numbers, company name, job title, etc.
