In the App Settings, you can set Call Forwarding, Voicemail, and some other features according to your needs.

To open the App Settings, you need to tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/372d31604f9aad756a4d/333869be89320abaaecc421555ae29a292946fce6ebab4b970aafde9f13fd3e1.png)icon.

The app settings are divided into two groups:

- Call Settings;

* Media and Files.

![](/img/ringotel/original/6d0ce70ad0094dc640c3/98fb27b98e21c5fb1ef316456d7f25dfe323b31a4285af3590ea8401bdafc088.png)

## Media and Files

Here you can set how to store call history, files, and pictures.
