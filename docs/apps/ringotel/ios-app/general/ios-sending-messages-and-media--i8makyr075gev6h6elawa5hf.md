## Sending a text message

To send a text message to another app user:

1. Open a chat with a colleague(s) you would like to send a message to.

![](/img/ringotel/original/1c0cd6fd2ee371501be6/98b53d7df5a8a539d65d68fc1ade4ed3121b82c5a9a010b0550de68552da67fe.png)

1. Tap on the text area at the bottom of the screen.
2. After you’ve finished typing your message, press the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/1ecb72eec15ddb023269/8efc7d22da2c89d092dffcd1ec89f74b4e170f32bce9e35b62ba73645c18591d.png)icon to send it.

![](/img/ringotel/original/158bc076859e36410235/8d636b33461f1167a381adde5cf1ded5f20327d604bda1019fda8a5c8d4fd64c.png)

## Sending files, photos, and locations

To send a file, photo, or your current location:

1. Open a chat with a colleague(s) you would like to send a file to.
2. Tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/cc44930326287ee60c38/c0a3a3da30abf5180132d794e5c7e4d31ad5bc0838f6814962ae20536598a250.png)icon at the bottom of the screen.

![](/img/ringotel/original/e4e40cf3102e5363c187/852949f50a769662723799f34a1a1c4a0a5592a2ec7218fd71ab8eaf9daa52e8.png)

1. Select the type of message that you would like to send.

![](/img/ringotel/original/4565ec3ba0ad61ac210e/30c605f3ef98402a55f018306d1c8023b99a914574e810997660e57aff33bcf9.png)
