There are several ways to initiate an outgoing call from the mobile app:

### Making a call from a contact profile

1. Open the profile of a contact that you would like to call.
2. Tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/984f76cc0178fe96febe/907aa9b8e209a375375671279651c01a147065210a2ddadb1ff26662da76829b.png)icon to make a call.

![](/img/ringotel/original/c56dc3b14984700f32e7/ef0383892a7856d1ca9a06962482b69ba6a19c29601794ca98c1c5e654122be6.png)

*User’s profile example*

![](/img/ringotel/original/8aad992fe769fd3217f0/e0ca3d244dd33871e97c130274173035111147eef58c407012089d4b2d272b73.png)

*External contact’s profile example*

### Making a call from a Keypad tab

1. Open the ‘**Keypad**’ tab by pressing the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/140fae854ca2089ce82e/27582fef43b624850b4b0519a90cc9506c69defb77026fca0c42b503969093d2.png)icon.
2. Dial a phone number manually or tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/2ed4425297b9673088b4/4c58022126ade37f7c2a26c377b2a59de746a1a5dd3fb90e86e7010ec4016ee5.png)icon to access your mobile device’s contacts list.

![](/img/ringotel/original/d950801451c68b52f0c5/cb8a1d79dcefc5e7530684524b99525276409959293807bff646df44daf1eae9.png)

### Making a call from a chat

1. Open the ‘**Calls**’ or ‘**Chats**’ tab.
2. Open a chat with a contact that you would like to call.
3. Press on a![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/49dc26b12c0c4680621d/6f9754fb2b13db6d799f43330f531296ae12235c70fd26efa0f67c33f5217c42.png)icon to make a call.

![](/img/ringotel/original/a7bee59de30010c31d81/8eb42a4d6ac4631cf99b4046f72f0b0936f0d0ec91b26050cc0ffca5e5f4d6b1.png)
