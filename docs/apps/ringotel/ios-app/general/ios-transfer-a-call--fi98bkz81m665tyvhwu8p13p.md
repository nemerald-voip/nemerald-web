*Looking for an&#x20;*[Android](/docs/apps/android-transfer-call--ukk2ikysb3a6guqd8gzugh87) *or&#x20;*[PC/Mac](/docs/apps/desktop-call-transfer--goxhybvy8g83rnreyy2bsadr) *version of this article?*

To transfer a call:

1. During an ongoing call, press the ‘**Transfer**’ icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/b427aeba262a7f68c9e5/d0e3ed4c40a8dd53aaf31cd446c20e5b6876e621b0629f5a2b498569901d8577.png)
2. Enter a phone number or choose a contact from the list, where you want to transfer a call.

![](/img/ringotel/original/edb8bfc351b898686012/0d0fca22298c3f4d424c3c6ff35f7c5841005312008f283c4d7a7ba75651b22e.png)

*1. Tap on the ‘Numpad’ icon to dial a phone number.*

1. Choose if you would like to do a Blind or Attended transfer.

![](/img/ringotel/original/a65f213d6061835daaaa/7c90b54691d7e34f4651cb03930259cb8996a7655bfef3eb9f41cc038f56333e.png)

If you choose a ‘**Blind transfer**’, you will be disconnected from this call while the caller will be transferred to the number you selected.

In the case of ‘**Attended transfer**’, the caller will be put on hold, while the app will make an outgoing call to the number/contact you chose to transfer a call to.

![](/img/ringotel/original/34be4df9ff0292e0f5b5/dee8f68a5270725e4eddd2fd99f424bacbbbd7fbfcde4ee5f38949baf1c63857.png)

1. Press the highlighted ‘**Transfer**’ button (1) to confirm the call transfer.
