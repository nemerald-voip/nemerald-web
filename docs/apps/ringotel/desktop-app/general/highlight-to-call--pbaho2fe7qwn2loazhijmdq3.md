The Highlight-To-Call feature allows you to highlight a phone number anywhere on the computer screen and automatically make a call via the Softphone app on Windows.

**How does it work?**

1. Your desktop app must be launched and logged in with your user account.
2. Highlight a phone number in any native app, like a web browser, Word, Acrobat Reader (PDF), etc.
3. To dial the highlighted number via the desktop app, press the**Pause/Break**button or**CTRL+F6**combination.
