During a call, you can transfer a call, put a call on hold, create a conference, and much more. You can access those features by utilizing the UI components described below:

![](/img/ringotel/original/0dee3c0ddd49ba174cf2/4da9706446d465242903f6c54c492779d2b3981fdbf117abc437b989c0c42c82.png)

1. **Call panel**. You can access a call panel by clicking on a contact you’re currently talking to in the list of contacts, chats, or calls, or by double-clicking on an Active Line.
2. **Active lines panel**. This is where all your active calls will be displayed.
3. **Pop-up window**. This panel will automatically pop up if a contact you’re talking to is not selected in the tabs panel.

## Put a call on hold

To put a call on hold:

Click the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/20b9951c40318cd0cea0/7a60b4fbf45ac99e322082f446ada037afe4c9ea8caae4b199e0fb7127071ed5.png)icon on the **Call panel&#x20;**&#x6F;r in the **Pop-up window**.

**OR**

Click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/7c10737329d19d59e3b4/6c4a75a00c5a57de9bded1029d1a4493fc8c2d6b76f174e4f44f4d38ed45f528.png)icon in the **Active Lines&#x20;**&#x70;anel and choose **Hold&#x20;**&#x66;rom the dropdown menu.

![](/img/ringotel/original/ca645105f48ab1f87cc8/76d3af86f6e446f255e420704bcbf855d6a8d88c15e179591bfdda84e2f15576.png)

![](/img/ringotel/original/18a6a60c91665cc39abd/b9a1adfeddedd63ad8ed21024b7ece34601c41d02928bdd4b2ffc9df54eec120.png)

To resume a call from hold:

Click the same icon again to resume.

![](/img/ringotel/original/f1d40fdc8f8cbde30b70/b7626c31c270b6eda8529f58a1ab498ef30fc95f163424ce18628de1604366cd.png)

## How to send DTMF-signals

Click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/267169cff00d5dc16b24/855593d528cbc7bb44e2f0069f0c091db771747633a4821196a7f33580dd550f.png)icon on the Call panel or at the Pop-up window to open the dial-pad. You can use it to send a DTMF signal during a call.

**Please, note that the dial pad from the Search Bar area doesn’t transmit DTMF signals.**

## Turn personal call recording on/off \*

To turn the call recording on/off during a call:

Click the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/12868436f7b0c680892b/ce093d3ad7d58d0c8de5d3f34c95db081c4363a3ca10ea023dcd4ad7fd53a19e.png)icon on a Call panel or Pop-up window.

**OR**

Click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/7c10737329d19d59e3b4/6c4a75a00c5a57de9bded1029d1a4493fc8c2d6b76f174e4f44f4d38ed45f528.png)icon of an Active Line and select **Recording&#x20;**&#x66;rom the drop-down menu.

*\* Please check the availability of this feature with your PBX Administrator.*
