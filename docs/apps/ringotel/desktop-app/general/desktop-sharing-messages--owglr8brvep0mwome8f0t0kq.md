To share a message, file, or call record from one chat to another:

1. Left-click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/18774bc3b39f63765a2b/87e5207e9e9ab7299d7a15aa0fc59baae11cf020e4198862c18773d8eca9bae7.png)icon above the message, file, or call record you would like to forward.
2. Select Share from the drop-down menu.

![](/img/ringotel/original/aa69ff58d34dfe2bb38b/31d2411ef9882e07ea3398782e6edb86330591c7bfb60f6e32a9479ef4793f0d.png)

1. Choose contacts or a group chat you want to forward a message to from the list and click Share.

![](/img/ringotel/original/c8bac2879d7fdfcac920/b262faef539625c0a0afc5be1e1ebd58da64e90f6231a6d71c2e762a55d232da.png)
