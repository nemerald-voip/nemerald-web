In case when you’re unable to pick up a call, you can send it to a voicemail. It can be enabled in your desktop app or by your PBX administrator.

To enable voicemail:

1. Go to the Menu tab.
2. Select **Settings**.

![](/img/ringotel/original/b9c9f962d9e8ec9c59ce/2a133c5045d936d24af0e62e532ed7fea1e07d55fd7482c8543d79f935fa02a7.png)

1. In the **Call&#x20;**&#x73;ettings tab toggle the Voicemail switch ON.

![](/img/ringotel/original/e5d39108153d025e5e15/780db8857ea512545bfca08366dbcc0484bee541ebae5fab34e07fb0e8c20019.png)

## Set voicemail greeting

To record a voicemail greeting:

1. Click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/12e28b7b1150065c146b/ddd1c38f4ffabaa9165d19e6ced5b4b526799209ed72c861c6d938ec65b01b48.png)icon.
2. In the opened window click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/4beb51e5406671873e3a/7d879ddd16d4e09a1e9eeb9896d78d356061418c3c4915acb0deb27226df50ce.png)button to start and stop recording after you finish.

![](/img/ringotel/original/683a97f4fa08cac7388b/2598dc6a1c9b63e6b96929fd2d5d735754bfa7fc67ce9202b85ea0bd6b8d80ea.png)

1. Click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/206806b408c8d7a572a6/8589bdb920b0e16214181b01dbd39edae0569557f919374e846a07156be6b811.png)icon to save the recorded greeting.

If the greeting isn’t recorded, an automated message will be prompted to a caller: “The following number \*your extension number\* is currently not available. Please call again or leave a message after a signal.”

If the Voicemail feature is turned on, your voicemail messages will be displayed in personal chat with a user who called like this:
