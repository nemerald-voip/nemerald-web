*Looking for an&#x20;*[iOS](/docs/apps/remove-participants-from-a-conference-ios--sauzezxvntgq18g8ddsip3t9) *or&#x20;*[*Android*](/docs/apps/remove-participants-from-a-conference-android--u3ni2o03p2evicpl2b4o7r6l) *version of this article?*

To remove participant from a conference:

1. During an ongoing conference call, click on the "Remove participant" button to open the list of participants.

![](/img/ringotel/original/8085e1b56f35d6532481/9fe23557ad473758ff33bbc676486221b0595b6afc28a3362bf1565772672281.png)

2\. In the participant list, hover over the user or contact you want to disconnect from the conference, then click the three dots and in the opened menu select **Drop call**.

![](/img/ringotel/original/f1b1ba02bb367461de48/e63c5e3b4dc244000614dffb55b7f90533374f45fe5b4b79ed5b75c1c224c186.png)
