Replying with a quotation is used when you want to reply to a specific message in a chat.

To reply with a quotation:

1. Left-click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/87534e2cfb859bacc11a/d011db380b7f7470ef5fc4cd98330c64abefdb1005b03f6d19e533f64df7b4c9.png)icon above the message to which you would like to reply.

![](/img/ringotel/original/874a097d254409bea73e/848a4e1c479be99b3cb6b479c7d48d0217ac84914eeaa5baf9c54e70bef4b371.png)

1. You will be able to see the message to which you are replying at the top of the **Message Input Area**.

![](/img/ringotel/original/e438d65718b29f549418/601004b8e007e1a08399df57dccf6e3677ce2730aa6c3a99a46eb129dd69b688.png)

1. Write your message and press **Enter&#x20;**&#x74;o send it.

Press ESC or click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/fe73c298831637761ce6/5d18013c048192d9e1200bc50dbd9879b1c08b42faf87c0a7bc60049216e9b61.png)icon to cancel the reply action.

You can jump to the replied message by clicking on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/dda23121560d2ae07c86/437831327a2ea9784e916c9879daab56d52cde755e008b3c4ad4fe3703f8efd6.png)icon:

![](/img/ringotel/original/8fc078ea9d58aa604f79/b86919fcb2c8d90da52a377c64d7841869ac0d8dbf0ec4a32dceb3206f5b8d6d.png)
