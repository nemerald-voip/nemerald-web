Speed dial list can contain phone numbers for a one-click call and indicators showing states of various features of your phone system.

![](/img/ringotel/original/8d49a97d15e2a783d314/810b47f39cbb32e8c5688cb0d8baccbf7bf1a649ab8ea2a44d9780ea581f40d9.png)

You can add any contact or a phone number to your speed dial list. To do so:

1. Click on the plus button![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/cc1a7dcd36a2aa8941be/732f38ce89390294a03c02465fd0e412e5db687c082496a41277c5dcd6738c66.png)at the right side of the tab name.
2. Choose the **Speed dial list&#x20;**&#x66;rom a drop-down menu.

![](/img/ringotel/original/e728c2e4be771e2d85e2/413da951140d6e1e30b2a90789639596fc73de2696e0cd9ad1d2ca29e51ca914.png)

1. In an **Add Number window&#x20;**&#x73;elect which contact or user you would like to add to the list by clicking on it.

![](/img/ringotel/original/d6b9e56b47aff6912c0d/105552b1d2ee6ec5c2137fd28a10094adfdd4f120a36c7588b62300d02ba3f50.png)

**OR**

1. Enter a new phone number in the search field and click on the **New number&#x20;**&#x69;tem below to add it to the speed dial.

![](/img/ringotel/original/dfcc22ec22709f455016/f43f2e9085e13e322014e199e192a9434d1eb903bdeae16fcb3c7d214371588d.png)
