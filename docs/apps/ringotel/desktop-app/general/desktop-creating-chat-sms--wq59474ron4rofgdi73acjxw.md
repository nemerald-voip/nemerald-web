To create a chat or send an SMS:

1. Click on an![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/cc1a7dcd36a2aa8941be/732f38ce89390294a03c02465fd0e412e5db687c082496a41277c5dcd6738c66.png)icon at the right side of the tab name.
2. Choose **Create Chat&#x20;**&#x66;rom a drop-down menu.
3. Select a **User&#x20;**&#x66;rom the list to create a personal cha&#x74;**.**

**OR**

1. Enter a phone number in the search field and choose an external contact to create an SMS chat. \*
