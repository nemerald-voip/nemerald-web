To view or edit contact details, click on the icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/a1149894ac0ed3faefa5/4a573baca9b53dddc20964106f085aeffc0f6d378f5620306eb374f7ebe832a3.png)in the upper right corner of the chat panel.

![](/img/ringotel/original/a1ab92e8c23f8efccf4b/8a4e495d5bd979bdb5deb3a78cd155f5ddaecc83815ee9e185f0ae1101d48ced.png)

A contact’s profile can contain all information about a contact - name, company, job title, phone, email, etc.

To edit information about the contact, click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/7c10737329d19d59e3b4/6c4a75a00c5a57de9bded1029d1a4493fc8c2d6b76f174e4f44f4d38ed45f528.png)icon and select the “Edit contact” button in the drop-down menu.

![](/img/ringotel/original/c51f5d7302d71d724051/5a30d80ef0405a6aa719fd0db8344d334ce9ced6b1d0d2e76f2c5c329d959792.png)

![](/img/ringotel/original/07d2b6b30f93839d0d6b/bc690fdff7fd719dacce48123cee79a5ac95ed5ce651d1a8052e435539db75c1.png)

After you’ve finished editing a contact’s details, click the **Save button&#x20;**&#x74;o save changes.

Or click on an![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/9fc6469043a79ded0fd5/00bba6f3d2d8fde1cffedb212380978dfbcd06db5b2e33c996675c6e07989724.png)icon to close contact details and discard the changes you made.
