You can add personal external contacts to your contacts list. The details of such contacts will be available to you only.

To add a personal contact:

1. Click the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/cc1a7dcd36a2aa8941be/732f38ce89390294a03c02465fd0e412e5db687c082496a41277c5dcd6738c66.png)icon on the right of the tab name.
2. In the drop-down menu choose “Create contact”.

![](/img/ringotel/original/e8917fdc26059f49984c/d146467fc580010ee2f0bec22beef45d12449dbf208fcc7ea2137507b72265bc.png)

1. In the **New Contact window**, enter the contact’s name and any other details.

You can add any type of information about your contact by clicking the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/0dad683fefada70bf6c4/35e3087522cedb2027a7e827732400fbc11799d6ecd64491f357efc949918b4b.png)icon.

![](/img/ringotel/original/ae0c3563e04d4bcae8c0/ad514017caf9a82e1a1cfa0334c2f9ef07eaef074c9c972e0af305a621da1208.png)

To delete a personal contact:

1. Click the right mouse button on the external contact you would like to delete while in the “Contacts” Tab.
2. In the drop-down menu choose the “Delete” option.
