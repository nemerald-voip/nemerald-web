## Blind transfer

To transfer a call:

1. Click the **Transfer&#x20;**&#x69;con![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/2fcd644d306a1713c1d3/f7b680ab582ff357c4dd29238083c600ad7fadf4ee16657c32f33701d52cf05d.png)on a [Call panel or Pop-up window](/docs/apps/desktop-call-hold-dtmf-recording--ybm4k618ragnlgyy963xbwpe) .

**OR**

1. Click on the menu icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/7c10737329d19d59e3b4/6c4a75a00c5a57de9bded1029d1a4493fc8c2d6b76f174e4f44f4d38ed45f528.png)of an [Active Line](/docs/apps/desktop-call-hold-dtmf-recording--ybm4k618ragnlgyy963xbwpe) and select **Transfer&#x20;**&#x66;rom the drop-down menu.
2. Choose a contact you want to transfer a call to from the list or enter a new phone number in a **Search field&#x20;**&#x61;nd click on the **Blind transfer&#x20;**&#x69;con.

![](/img/ringotel/original/510820b95d2f6a9c0b89/a994894570060a0dd8bcf484b68284a045485dd00f79193ada8f7ec3ed67db19.png)

## Attended (warm) transfer

To make an attended (warm) transfer:

1. Follow steps 1 and 2 like for Blind Transfer but press the **Attended transfer&#x20;**&#x62;utton instead.

An additional active panel will appear for the second active call while the first active call will be put on hold automatically.

1. Click on the **Transfer&#x20;**&#x62;utton at **Active Line&#x20;**&#x6F;r **Pop-up window**.

## Alternatively

You can also transfer a call in the following way:

1. During an active call, initiate a new [outgoing call](/docs/apps/desktop-outgoing-call--te63hnixsyugdk7wpsw59xeq) . The current call will be placed on hold automatically.
2. Click on the **Transfer&#x20;**&#x62;utton at [Active Line or Pop-up window](/docs/apps/desktop-call-hold-dtmf-recording--ybm4k618ragnlgyy963xbwpe) .
