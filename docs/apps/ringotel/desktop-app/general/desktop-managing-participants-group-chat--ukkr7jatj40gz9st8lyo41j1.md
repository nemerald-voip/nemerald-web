## Adding/removing users in a group chat

Only administrators and the ones who created the group can add or remove participants.

To add/remove a participant:

1. Open a Chat Details panel by clicking on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/a1149894ac0ed3faefa5/4a573baca9b53dddc20964106f085aeffc0f6d378f5620306eb374f7ebe832a3.png)icon at the top right corner of a chat panel.
2. Click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/998120e61e11e08b59c3/c9ed96a841b9a98e63fc322867cdeb101ed5f21856c7a89003b9a3108f3773d6.png)icon.

![](/img/ringotel/original/4c2db79fbe8c0a96dabe/51db23ffbdde3ae913fa639b72d0543615f067bf6169e309233ded5f8ff87c14.png)

1. Click on users to add or the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/c53798b8b683fe32f0ea/5b84cd9d1a06b05a7e52dafe67eac9cef9dee331fab07e958d87e3596c06e323.png)icon next to their names to **remove&#x20;**&#x74;hem from a group chat.

![](/img/ringotel/original/4d98368c85a3937b5554/225a6df2a51256aa9143aaeeecc25132cbfb3fdc2e36032686aa34724570f8b3.png)

1. Click on the **Save&#x20;**&#x62;utton.

## Adding admins to a group chat

You can assign **Chat Admins&#x20;**&#x6F;nly to a group chat that you have created or during the chat creation process. A Chat Admin can add and remove participants in a group chat.

To assign a Chat Admin:

1. Open a **Chat Details&#x20;**&#x70;anel by clicking on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/a1149894ac0ed3faefa5/4a573baca9b53dddc20964106f085aeffc0f6d378f5620306eb374f7ebe832a3.png)icon at the top right corner of a chat panel.
2. Click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/18774bc3b39f63765a2b/87e5207e9e9ab7299d7a15aa0fc59baae11cf020e4198862c18773d8eca9bae7.png)icon next to the participant you want to make a chat admin.
3. Select **Administrator&#x20;**&#x66;rom the drop-down menu to assign or remove admin privileges.
