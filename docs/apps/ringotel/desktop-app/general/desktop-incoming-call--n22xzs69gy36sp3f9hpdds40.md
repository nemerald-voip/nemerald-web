When you receive an incoming call:

1. An **Active Lines&#x20;**&#x70;anel will appear under the **Search Bar**.

![](/img/ringotel/original/70cdd795f7505280431f/17bf196f883bb0428b5687a8f36a6c0e8c7024ab98f894bdd052de21e075e511.png)

1. Click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/81560df075ca5bb3361a/06a244a0a248024766ca3024966078a16fed0a705429dc65e96af9ed0604f51b.png)icon, to answer a call, or the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/29a3f40d7ae637553b4c/c448eaab313dde04a1f605e30f05a5239392d51f7eb6ba3ac6b03095028482c7.png)icon, to drop it.

If you have an open chat with a caller during an incoming call, a Call panel will appear at the top of the Chat panel.

![](/img/ringotel/original/6a3b87fbb60f31e33510/bf0fe0ca0b3db963772aa33af96243c8688eabffd123663874cfe4c26e77d551.png)

On incoming calls, a Call panel has the following buttons:

![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/7bfa9619924f3a8d8e81/67484b11679492c46a5d197b904192aafbeff57fc851ee2bda0880e91e5204cf.png)- to mute a ringtone,

![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/81560df075ca5bb3361a/06a244a0a248024766ca3024966078a16fed0a705429dc65e96af9ed0604f51b.png)- to answer a call with audio,

![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/107d62424713ae87d613/a708b9d7702e349214a6fefa651becf689d0ea74fc1be514c7034fb6d17e7cbd.png)- to answer with a video on,

![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/29a3f40d7ae637553b4c/c448eaab313dde04a1f605e30f05a5239392d51f7eb6ba3ac6b03095028482c7.png)- to cancel it.

If you have other applications on top of your screen when receiving an incoming call, you can handle an incoming call with the help of a notification window that will automatically pop up.

Depending on the option selected in [General Settings](/docs/apps/desktop-general-app-settings--g4g5p30bgtm5ofmc1sxsa040) , a pop-up window will have one of the following looks:

![](/img/ringotel/original/119698b6c9bf6a9ba2c9/7bd466ff41c55495fe495e4ce1c7cfc8d5e3e399049b4c1e23fdd319c865db9b.png)
