You can view the history of all your incoming and outgoing calls through the [Calls tab](/docs/apps/desktop-user-interface--ophgjz3guikdjfl0oga2h6t5) .

Click on the arrow icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/13fb944daf95b1f8924c/15361f8bd8cf1dda4fa9fcda1c9823b1cd068acd88d2a6883d4c8dd15fcb6083.png)to switch between displaying all calls and only missed ones.

![](/img/ringotel/original/3afd92dbaf164d2df150/ed2527c1f56ad13346d62617cf12a7ea657fac3e7219b274f47da27cf7bc4fd3.png)

## Delete call history

To remove a call from history right-click on the call entry and select Delete call from the drop-down menu.

![](/img/ringotel/original/542e380a2c595984342a/addbcc12bc191a9e2d147b33cdfc11e68cc95ff441606e41111e55391e49021d.png)

To remove multiple calls from your call history at once:

1. Right-click on any call record.
2. Click on Select multiple from the drop-down menu.
3. Select the records you would like to delete. You can click on a specific date to select all entries from that date.
4. Click on **Delete&#x20;**&#x74;o remove entries.
