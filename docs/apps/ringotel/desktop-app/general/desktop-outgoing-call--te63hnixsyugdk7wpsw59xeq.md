There are several ways you can make an outgoing call from the desktop app:

**OPTION 1**

1. **Right-click&#x20;**&#x6F;n a contact, chat, or call record in any of the tabs.
2. Select Dial from the drop-down menu to make an audio call.

![](/img/ringotel/original/baae641bdf8d020be390/4b9ee501f9bad912267232e21e9b249858d2cb18fa6e62c154df2f5d2209b073.png)

**OPTION 2**

1. **Left-click&#x20;**&#x6F;n a contact, chat, or call record to open a chat.
2. Click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/15b7b4545e7baeb6c1f6/8184015fda112d6829f5edccd9131a16385dbf1826908036caccbf2ba593ad4b.png)icon to initiate an audio call or click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/f9e7bc348ca940a431a2/c0ef03e9a9b6cf1b2c927606e2327678bee0496366929d76ef71fb104b72ba1f.png)icon to initiate a video call (available only for Users).

![](/img/ringotel/original/cccfbd2b83aa49e16226/9693768bbb6fe301c1da6d93435f54c3f6f99ddaa8c692f123f276a756d23bb0.png)

**OPTION 3**

1. Enter a contact’s name or phone number in the **Search bar**.
2. Press Enter or the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/aabcd8b4d1a3aa06b2b4/ef86d543f0c8f03bfb41e4faf5f5783cb995b93a111758f308b60ef67fa82c72.png)icon to make an audio call.

![](/img/ringotel/original/f719c17697715cdccd1d/ba9066d1d22c3a0fb3060c4cd056edbe4d3091afad7e685efee923c34120d8a9.png)

**OR**

Open the dial pad by clicking on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/64a011f113f729ebf9e5/1b73071c716acfdafb59b0cc097ffd95afcb70b7bf6e43694ad71e3af912903f.png)icon in the Search field to call extensions or external numbers.

![](/img/ringotel/original/bfe6cf2c8a255b750b70/0c87f914e42ece975454c7f8ee8ce2e179a594b40a078e3c8b21b3990c931978.png)
