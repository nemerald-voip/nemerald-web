*Looking for an&#x20;*[Android](/docs/apps/android-blocklist--lg90ce3ygm72r214logejh7k) *or&#x20;*[iOS](/docs/apps/ios-blocklist--lchl96vtphrmdtytkicr7qo8) *version of this article?*

You can block incoming calls and SMS from the selected contacts and phone numbers.

**NOTE:**&#x42;locked contacts are shared across your entire organisation, so if you block a number, it will also appear on other users' blocked list.

## How to block a new contact

To block a new number:

1. Navigate to Menu > Blocked.
2. In the text field, enter the phone number, then click on that number under the "New contact" header.
3. In the appeared pop-up, optionally specify the contact name and the reason for blocking this number.
4. Press the "Block" button to confirm.

![](/img/ringotel/original/68aa19c74c77ac3d24e2/21c7a2e61462b0558a56ee7acdca9b8169477998ce890058c5d04e6215b2f558.png)

## How to block an existing contact

To block an existing contact:

1. Navigate to Contacts tab.
2. Find and select the contact in the list.
3. In the contact profile, click on the three-dots menu and select "Block".
4. Alternatively, right-click on the record in the contact list and in the context menu select "Block".

![](/img/ringotel/original/8b50af12012f7c3f92e1/b8991dbbf81dc9b8224a793c569179ddc42f352fa49cdfbceb2652e33ee57d31.png)

## How to unblock a contact

To unblock a blocked contact:

1. Navigate to Menu > Blocked.
2. Find and select the contact in the list.
3. In the blocked contact profile, click the "Unblock" button.

![](/img/ringotel/original/b618cff3e3b44f77a96b/c848a9eacaccee2397a3acf8c05db68076846c80fadf19ebe48899b3980a341c.png)
