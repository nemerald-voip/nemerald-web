*Looking for an*[*iOS*](/docs/apps/ios-start-working-with-mobile-app--jxyp3rlfbawvxahjpky05p62)*or*[*Android*](/docs/apps/android-start-working-with-mobile-app--bhomyeazhcleknx00v59e4na)*version of this article?*

To start using the application on your desktop device, you need to:

1. Open an email from your PBX Administrator that contains links for downloading the apps, your authentication credentials, and a QR code for easy access to your account.

![](/img/ringotel/original/7d66016e59d78356bda1/2d7179b0bd972a6338fcfd81d4eaea7cba0d63d67d1765f27a7545a80eb8b1ca.png)

1. Click on the icon to download the app suitable for the Operating System on your desktop device.
2. After installing the app on your device, click on the app icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/575027402949337630e0/41b98c71a6c5c1c0832ac8ba70fb6b88281f80f3f3f9cef5d5b13916e5def31c.png)to launch the app.
3. To log into the app, you can enter your Authentication Credentials provided in the email you received from your PBX Administrator.
