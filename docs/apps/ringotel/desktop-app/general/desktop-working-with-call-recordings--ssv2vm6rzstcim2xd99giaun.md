*Looking for an&#x20;*[*iOS*](/docs/apps/ios-listening-sharing-recording--s99acnxuit527m1nl2j386zg) *or&#x20;*[*Android*](/docs/apps/android-listening-sharing-recording--x7bkxmsy46nq43zq88sa8noh) *version of this article?*

**To playback a call recording**:

1. Open your conversation history with a contact.
2. Find a call you would like to listen to and press the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/92be778f8f70c2ed28b4/1a9c0951d18d87c7e269e211587604ca5d60ec3b42c65f796ab7435c55709051.png)icon.

![](/img/ringotel/original/53ef114c2287cee61a23/03898c26164e52d60f57d52872d95eca8081aa0149d9dd1837daa3661a7c21df.png)

**To download a call recording**:

1. Open your conversation history with a contact.
2. Find a call recording that you would like to download and press the **View Details&#x20;**&#x6C;ink.
3. Click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/aaea83b3af41c2fa3303/cdcc260ea59d09a90283a563443ad4557157fdfe1a119fb54e1357191e88f0e3.png)icon to save this call’s recording to your computer.

![](/img/ringotel/original/3adf986f3434377d080e/b38ec193d7fa64d5a467bd572283b55404b1e4a821571e2247095e35a63e6dd5.png)

*\* Please check the availability of this feature with your PBX Administrator.*
