To remove a message:

1. Click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/18774bc3b39f63765a2b/87e5207e9e9ab7299d7a15aa0fc59baae11cf020e4198862c18773d8eca9bae7.png)icon near the message you want to delete.
2. Select **Delete&#x20;**&#x66;rom the drop-down menu to remove a message.

![](/img/ringotel/original/50e1f3226828067e2d4d/6128fe82425f99ee2291d6b6f3f4109a12ca3ef61ef16b1f96d3d936f2129157.png)

To remove chat history or a chat itself:

1. Click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/7c10737329d19d59e3b4/6c4a75a00c5a57de9bded1029d1a4493fc8c2d6b76f174e4f44f4d38ed45f528.png)icon near the username or chat name.
2. Select **Delete chat&#x20;**&#x6F;r **Delete messages&#x20;**&#x66;rom the drop-down menu accordingly.

![](/img/ringotel/original/ce2b13e0cd8dd4ad20ef/371207d1614d0751393cd062ace6d22c76adb15af6eb82278cdb275f0f070c2a.png)
