To start using the app on your Mac or Windows PC, click on the app icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/575027402949337630e0/41b98c71a6c5c1c0832ac8ba70fb6b88281f80f3f3f9cef5d5b13916e5def31c.png)in the launchpad (Mac) or Start menu (Windows). Then enter the authentication parameters in the email you received from your PBX Admin, as shown below.

![](/img/ringotel/original/7ce6ea4adbe60f56a29e/511737db3dd73f75b73b039acb62001fb70674cec4cfa00d272c518feb0490ce.png)

After you have entered your domain name, click the **Login&#x20;**&#x62;utton to start the app.

If you're using a Proxy server to access the Internet, click on the **Settings&#x20;**&#x62;utton to open the app's proxy settings:

![](/img/ringotel/original/2825629aa65afba6daaf/dcfec69ffbc45038c08453dbaf5a0e1e18b70cbd531f3555308e85bcfc8fa07b.png)

*In case of any errors occurring during the authentication process - please check if the information you entered is correct and if you have a stable internet connection.*
