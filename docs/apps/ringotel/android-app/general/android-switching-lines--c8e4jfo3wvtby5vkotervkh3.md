During a call, if you need to make an outgoing call while putting the current call on hold:

1. Tap on the ‘**More**’ icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/a08f7294872c8d762df8/685ce6a6c868a9af192200e4ce52d00935cc8a61a0d9e5382fa7207d7c241dbf.png)to switch to the additional features set and press the ‘**Add call**’ icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/94a5a1b6db1e872deea2/93d909b9212bdbc8a387fc18e028ff5327484508960a309c0347ba8df9d908dc.png)

![](/img/ringotel/original/3466f4d4d402f6414df0/5da6a9cb8100072b0ff7a4c5be44877f045e58d627cebf5cd9f3c48f6acc682c.png)

1. Choose а contact that you’d like to call from the list.

![](/img/ringotel/original/81fb86be9fdb38a15ef7/614ff1cc8e808f1358506eaa56d562adc0eca4b4f6e94de922b80fc92a466e86.png)

*i. Tap the ‘Numpad’ icon to manually dial a phone number.*
