Upon receiving an incoming call, on your mobile device, you will see the following screen:

![](/img/ringotel/original/9a0580c443ce392b36b2/302712a03b8a40784490d6113005fc725c1765516d3dc9e5aaaebf8c5aeb84e2.png)

*1. Drop a call*

*2. Pick up with a video on*

*3. Pick up with audio only*

During a call, you have access to the following features:

![](/img/ringotel/original/fcb3d17fc060efedadea/5d8ee3c4eb35beea8c58976af114b2aa02b92a70518db26ccde01a86f57641bb.png)

*1. Mute / unmute microphone*

*2. Put the call on hold*

*3. Turn the speaker on / off, switch to Bluetooth*

*4. Open the Numpad to send DTMF*

*5. Turn call recording on / off*

*6. Open a contact's profile*

*7. Transfer a call*

*8. Make a parallel call*

*9. Turn video on / off*

*10. Open a chat with the contact*
