When you make dozens of calls every day, it is very hard to remember the details of every single call. By filling in a call subject and adding some comments, you can highlight the most important parts of your conversation and easily find them later, when you’ll need them.

**To view/add information about a call**:

1. Open your conversation history with a contact. You can do that by switching to the ‘**Calls**’ tab and selecting a contact from the list of your calls.

![](/img/ringotel/original/f05f0315f06892ed4cba/9bfae6286f44850dcc9f1f5db5e1682b650baa45d1c32afa160f24f1ab4bb19e.png)

1. After selecting a contact, you’ll see a history of all communication with that contact. Tap on a ‘**View details**’ link of a call you’d like to add details to.

![](/img/ringotel/original/c7caf5a588d125ecc43e/d06a527b790726eef862e187d8fa23fb4d528c2ec4e7be890e9ff2f61bcdbecf.png)

## Adding Call Subject and comments

When you open the ‘**Call details**’ panel, you can view and edit the subject of a call and write out some important parts of a conversation in the ‘**Comments**’ section.

After you finish editing the Call Details, tap on the "Save" button to confirm.

![](/img/ringotel/original/80938506fb924f86411c/5ac5b312d0fffc74243dcfa4d44b2edb93626b9c6fa0c82e5dca50c8cd40c793.png)

You can also tap on an![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/4bb192a2f753df63174f/a0c0237a7dbc43b4157d8d0f539afcfb93600729aad93dc79691f49916285611.png)icon to mark a call as a ‘**Favorite**’ which will make it easier to find.

## Finding a call by its subject

Specifying the subject of a call can help you easily find that call any time later.

To find a call by its subject, simply type a subject you want to find in the Chat Search field and press the ‘**Search**’ button.

![](/img/ringotel/original/17692dc4c10ac6eec70a/1683f7cefbee8eb578d74e08ff099e7143d3168d0f7cfaaff131a0186fc10a1d.png)
