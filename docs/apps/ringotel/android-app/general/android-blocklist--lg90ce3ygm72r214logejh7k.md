You can block incoming calls and SMS from the selected contacts and phone numbers.

**NOTE:**&#x42;locked contacts are shared across your entire organisation, so if you block a number, it will also appear on other users' blocked list.

## How to add a phone number to Block list

To add a new phone number to your Block list:

1. Navigate to Menu > Blocked.
2. Tap the "Add" button.
3. On the Create contact screen, specify the phone number and (optionally) the reason for blocking this number.
4. Press the Save button to confirm.

## How to block an existing contact

To block an existing contact:

1. Navigate to Contacts tab.
2. Find and select the contact in the list.
3. In the contact profile, tap on the menu icon and select the Block option.

![](/img/ringotel/original/72fb2013764e4ba8af0a/2756c25747a3e088ac37ab32762c8de9829c2601506811ea3d81dc226d015160.png)

## How to unblock a contact

To unblock a blocked contact:

1. Navigate to Menu > Blocked.
2. Find and select the contact in the list.
3. In the pop-up menu press the "Remove" button or open the contact details and unblock in from its profile.

![](/img/ringotel/original/0a3543b8fc3ecb4dff1e/30534b65db612326c2658256b6cca14a8d2878a85f2d49328d00d6397a060e94.png)
