To remove participant from a conference:

1. During an ongoing conference call, tap on the “Remove participant” button at the top-right corner of the app to open the list of participants.

![](/img/ringotel/original/929819bd9fdc5313817f/5cf285b4de58bcabd7290ebc4b99f98f33cda8fedbe2ee81431582c35c60c3ab.png)

2\. In the participant list, select the user or contact you want to disconnect from the conference.

![](/img/ringotel/original/3073bc7038dfc75fe184/685315cdaa2e9f78f2acb9d17e0282ed524ef1dfef9121d2246c4a05abf0576d.png)
