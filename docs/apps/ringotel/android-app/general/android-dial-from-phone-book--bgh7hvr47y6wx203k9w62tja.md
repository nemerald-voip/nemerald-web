To call a phone number that is in your device’s contacts list and is not imported into the mobile app:

1. Switch to the ‘**Contacts**’ tab and tap on the ‘**Phone**’ icon.

![](/img/ringotel/original/1d1381cb49035cf4d376/5623a526ded50a5c20f31ddb9557f7946997e13db15ea1b6b96f0c911a932ca7.png)

1. On the screen with the list of your contacts, tap on a contact you want to call.
2. Press the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/59955a6f38616f1978e3/1f41bc2851a76982c16d148a314fcf12b1f4d0c1e601d70a89e45da2702d6de3.png)icon to make a call.

![](/img/ringotel/original/33fe0dda89569c5510dd/ee9aa80c0266ff2096d5d5ac441f9c2fa4a43f6d3a98e0d12df719eb535380b4.png)

**Alternatively**

1. Open the ‘Keypad’ tab
2. Press the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/a693d55d81f144f4dd6c/a06e05e90dff65ed275cc32822eba537b0e0777220f9de4ec4e51acde425494c.png)icon on the bottom left corner of the numpad.

![](/img/ringotel/original/247542ff88ed0eeee607/3bc7ad46ac4d783fe87a24661ad88e2b066b61fb635132de872ba78cae66ec67.png)
