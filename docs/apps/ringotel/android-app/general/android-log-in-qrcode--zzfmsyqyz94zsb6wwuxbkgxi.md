To use a QR code that you received in an email from your PBX Administrator:

1. Click on a QR-code icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/c82fc24abb46130d7d77/c97b0f613e539df264988784fad0542e5f4e62948900cc3cd7bdd040efedf669.png)at the login screen:

![](/img/ringotel/original/943ffd633a598f2236f3/6af5418d4a60653e8ba7b1ed616d838ab765b5f8f0e8ecb4b841adb262130eca.png)

1. Point your mobile device camera at the QR code in your email to scan the QR code.

![](/img/ringotel/original/8bd4f9cc7d2605cdf2e7/c5351a83f0e6ad7bd2e8b554f02c3adc0184af21119001a0e181d7cc15ab36c1.png)

**NOTE:**&#x57;ithout access to your device’s camera, the mobile app cannot read the QR code. To scan a QR code, the mobile app will ask permission to access your camera.
