*Looking for an&#x20;*[PC/Mac](/docs/apps/desktop-conference-calls--vaqva2dc377xnjga0me78u8p) *or&#x20;*[iOS](/docs/apps/ios-add-to-conference--pjkfi0mfezoimgar5qa4fzg0) *version of this article?*

To add a participant to a call:

1. Tap the ‘More’ icon during an ongoing call to switch to the additional features set.
2. Press the ‘**Add call**’ icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/94a5a1b6db1e872deea2/93d909b9212bdbc8a387fc18e028ff5327484508960a309c0347ba8df9d908dc.png)

![](/img/ringotel/original/3466f4d4d402f6414df0/5da6a9cb8100072b0ff7a4c5be44877f045e58d627cebf5cd9f3c48f6acc682c.png)

1. Choose а contact that you’d like to add to a call from the list.

![](/img/ringotel/original/70b14d4f6f28b8ea0d94/e8a9d349f3659cad2ded7354fbcd686b12f38199baeec9f3c20f228ccb502a7d.png)

*i. Tap the ‘Numpad’ icon to manually dial a phone number.*

1. After you’ve connected with a new participant, press the ‘Merge’ button to add this participant to a conference.

![](/img/ringotel/original/14582d4ec900002dc81f/e4adcbd3b18d9c3504d03eda710b5f2e34747c8e361fb7a358177bb34998293d.png)

*i. Press the ‘Merge’ button to merge both lines into a conference.*

You can repeat all the above steps to add more people to a conference call.
