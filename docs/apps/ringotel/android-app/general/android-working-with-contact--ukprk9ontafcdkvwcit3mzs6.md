A Contact’s Profile contains all the details regarding a person or an organization, including the history of communication with your company.

To open a contact’s profile, simply tap on a contact from your [contacts list](/docs/apps/android-contacts--icbab0zkyvel86v5acnif2wn) , or select a ‘**Contact Info**’ option from a dropdown menu on a chat screen.

2. Share contact.

## Edit a contact

To edit a contact tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/d404746dea3e7bbe0e32/c5dc0e365c7e0a5b48b11e90932007d994bbb6a8be3ded7bcd24e6c615397908.png)menu icon in the contact’s profile and choose **‘Edit’&#x20;**&#x66;rom a dropdown menu.

## Delete a contact

To delete a contact:

- Long-tap on a contact in the contacts list.

OR

- Tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/d404746dea3e7bbe0e32/c5dc0e365c7e0a5b48b11e90932007d994bbb6a8be3ded7bcd24e6c615397908.png)menu icon in the contact’s profile and choose **‘Delete’&#x20;**&#x66;rom a dropdown menu.

## Add a contact to favorites

To add a contact to favorites, open a profile of contact and tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/f0f3239479c3becd4e0f/d6c77988a0c46121019ee8d1209ede21cd8b27f447a55f6a7a0cc7e708c30629.png)icon.

## Share a contact

To share a contact with your colleagues:

1. Open the profile of a contact you want to share and tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/d404746dea3e7bbe0e32/c5dc0e365c7e0a5b48b11e90932007d994bbb6a8be3ded7bcd24e6c615397908.png)menu icon.
2. Select "**Share with**" from the dropdown menu.
3. Select colleagues or chat groups you would like to share this contact with.
4. Tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/752291190ae5263d1ef8/ededb9584a66211cbd4fcfa6389b91798c5882b9c00e39e186534c84a57e111b.png)icon to confirm and share the contact.
