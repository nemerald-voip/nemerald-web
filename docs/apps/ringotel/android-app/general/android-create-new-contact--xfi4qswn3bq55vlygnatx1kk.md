To create a new contact, tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/df13f2a1733f3c62bcf4/7477c311fda1a8364a7077f325721054d08f28eec224d5a7c916634070b53915.png)icon.

Then fill in the name of the contact and add additional details by tapping on the ‘**Add details**’ button.

![](/img/ringotel/original/b4409be3ee18eaacdb16/6f17599a62bb8c3b8d61c0fa7f2ab5e69419ab36d06f597e8d4c8bc2d8d586fc.png)

1. Press ‘Save’ to finish editing the contact.
2. Add an avatar picture to the contact.
3. Add details like phone numbers, company name, job title, etc.
