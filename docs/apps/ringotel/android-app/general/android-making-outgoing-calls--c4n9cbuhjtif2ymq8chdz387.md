There are several ways to initiate an outgoing call from the mobile app:

## Making a call from a contact profile

1. Open the [profile](/docs/apps/android-working-with-contact--ukprk9ontafcdkvwcit3mzs6) of a contact/user that you would like to call.
2. Tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/59955a6f38616f1978e3/1f41bc2851a76982c16d148a314fcf12b1f4d0c1e601d70a89e45da2702d6de3.png)icon to make a voice call or on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/912ec66971c418f74948/b7e934df4e1a559a0e07f472f7634cc351ca365b8aed3576395f92701c8cad89.png)icon to make video call.

![](/img/ringotel/original/bad703febca3911148e8/e6c67c5a3ed323d387c2188cae86b3c323d897dcd047793b80a646626d96411b.png)

*User’s profile example*

*External contact’s profile example*

## Making a call from a Calls tab

1. Open the ‘**Calls**’ tab

![](/img/ringotel/original/f6cbdd049ca4c7d88068/710d59c3b128123c9cbe7902076e3c8981218691173c43a11ccde34ee262da6d.png)

1. Select a contact from your call history.
2. Press on a![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/59955a6f38616f1978e3/1f41bc2851a76982c16d148a314fcf12b1f4d0c1e601d70a89e45da2702d6de3.png)icon to make a call.

![](/img/ringotel/original/ba856c41fbe4b787d6ff/6fdfba55519fe44f86fa9993dab0db76e4b586d65111dded79eca012d5a69763.png)

## Making a call from the Keypad

1. Open the Keypad tab.
2. Enter the phone number you'd like to call.

![](/img/ringotel/original/247542ff88ed0eeee607/3bc7ad46ac4d783fe87a24661ad88e2b066b61fb635132de872ba78cae66ec67.png)

1. Press on a![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/034436d2fd48894ebde0/715eb0a6dd851a107c5d075ecf9b4e56f43e917b0f349bbfe9ac4532873c46e8.png)button to make a call.
