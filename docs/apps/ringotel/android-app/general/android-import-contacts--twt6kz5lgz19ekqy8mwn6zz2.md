To import a contact from your Android device into the mobile app:

1. Tap on the ‘**Phone**’ icon in the ’**Contacts**’ tab.

![](/img/ringotel/original/4fbd3e285f6d41728906/3d34f6b85473986a15e542d1247bc15ec2808ea7ece2be3f4177325c4d4cf756.png)

1. Tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/91f055fa03b13c57b78c/cc539f3fb8944d128b72e89e7c5f787b1b3a7f7250d2bd692b7edc154405e6d3.png)icon to select the contacts you want to import.

![](/img/ringotel/original/dd83ceb4698f526a5a85/562259591efcc28b498545002db02d233fa7b9f3e9f49855272e1078ec0b7bfe.png)

1. Select the contacts you would like to import and press the ‘**Done**’ button to finish importing.

![](/img/ringotel/original/dd2e1680114ac3d62aee/5b703a6e7ab8d755b0f1f547683abd6b4ae80c05533fd83bb79ccb9cd119ec30.png)
