*Looking for an&#x20;*[*iOS*](/docs/apps/ios-start-working-with-mobile-app--jxyp3rlfbawvxahjpky05p62) *or&#x20;*[*PC/Mac*](/docs/apps/desktop-start-working-with-app--a7ufngmkbmmo2vbz2fpk7zne) *version of this article?*

To start using the mobile application on your Android device, you need to:

1. Open an email from your PBX Administrator that contains links for downloading the apps, your authentication credentials, and a QR code for easy access to your account.

![](/img/ringotel/original/7d66016e59d78356bda1/2d7179b0bd972a6338fcfd81d4eaea7cba0d63d67d1765f27a7545a80eb8b1ca.png)

1. Click on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/87811a832da39b3e7bec/63e4ae47e650d8a737d57fe14c69c5248306c1f6b8262cde28b70d68a2eb8c3e.png)icon to download the mobile app, suitable for your Android device.
2. After installing the app on your phone, click on the app icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/8f68f5dc25cb60a89123/304bad76481db3656455f4afae23ce35de2946d4030403ac0b2eef8dcd67f6a1.png)to launch the app.
3. To log into the app, you can enter your Authentication Credentials manually or scan a QR code provided in the email you received from your PBX Administrator.
