*Looking for an&#x20;*[iOS](/docs/apps/ios-transfer-a-call--fi98bkz81m665tyvhwu8p13p) *or&#x20;*[PC/Mac](/docs/apps/desktop-call-transfer--goxhybvy8g83rnreyy2bsadr) *version of this article?*

To transfer a call:

1. During an ongoing call, tap the ‘**More**’ icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/a08f7294872c8d762df8/685ce6a6c868a9af192200e4ce52d00935cc8a61a0d9e5382fa7207d7c241dbf.png)to switch to the additional features set.
2. Press the ‘**Transfer**’ icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/c5a3dde796f2a06f4be5/fcf621c5478444a658e1cdbba8e9a8a0a247c45e58259b3e06db5316ac6a1a44.png)

![](/img/ringotel/original/3466f4d4d402f6414df0/5da6a9cb8100072b0ff7a4c5be44877f045e58d627cebf5cd9f3c48f6acc682c.png)

1. Enter a phone number or choose a contact from the list, where you want to transfer a call.

![](/img/ringotel/original/81fb86be9fdb38a15ef7/614ff1cc8e808f1358506eaa56d562adc0eca4b4f6e94de922b80fc92a466e86.png)

*i. Tap on the ‘Numpad’ icon to manually dial a phone number.*

1. Choose if you would like to do a Blind or Attended transfer.

![](/img/ringotel/original/a5aa5b4557f3194099f8/918fff82c3250fa54cb4308da8c75003948bfced97daf4e2c585996a8fb85cf9.png)

If you choose a ‘**Blind transfer**’, you will be disconnected from this call while the caller will be transferred to the number you selected.

In the case of ‘**Attended transfer**’, the caller will be put on hold, while the app will make an outgoing call to the number/contact you chose to transfer a call to.

![](/img/ringotel/original/9a5498adfc20a1574723/a81d7a1cb97ee370607775861d8a0f6b929154d51b89cafac55410e1450c534d.png)

*i. Press the ‘Transfer’ button to confirm the call transfer.*
