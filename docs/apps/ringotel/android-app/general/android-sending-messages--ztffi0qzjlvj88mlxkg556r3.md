## Sending a text message

To send a text message to another app user:

1. Open a chat with a colleague(s) you would like to send a message to.
2. Tap on the text area at the bottom of the screen.
3. After you’ve finished typing your message, press the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/38ebfc91dccd26a6cff6/c41538069d3c67cf954a3fb5d8b1149a8c357208824c15d350cadf9c0aea101e.png)icon to send it.

## Sending an SMS message \*

To send an SMS message to a contact or a phone number:

1. In the [‘Chats’ tab](/docs/apps/android-chats-and-messages--wiert7wul43oyqrsq57amnnr) press on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/5c216d41c20b25c70472/39dd17861b340716bbd4622a4146d79ced0b8559501f20d8979465a49ccaa3ac.png)icon at the bottom of the screen and then tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/5c216d41c20b25c70472/39dd17861b340716bbd4622a4146d79ced0b8559501f20d8979465a49ccaa3ac.png)icon to create a new chat.
2. Select a contact you’d like to send an SMS message or type in a phone number at the top of the screen.

![](/img/ringotel/original/10d2c84a83a457f67395/c21da93068a3afd1441c80429386ec47b7606ccea104d8c411ed629ae6382dde.png)

1. Tap on the text area at the bottom of the screen and type in your message.

![](/img/ringotel/original/ca5ccf93a1a260bdb6cc/6926a8bd5a9312d3046e02d9e98c06ba6f07a7fcbfecf21c28125c75b0e79f84.png)

1. After you’ve finished typing your message, press the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/38ebfc91dccd26a6cff6/c41538069d3c67cf954a3fb5d8b1149a8c357208824c15d350cadf9c0aea101e.png)icon to send it.

*\* Please check the availability of this feature with your PBX Administrator.*

## Sending files, photos, and locations

To send a file, photo, or your current location:

1. Open a chat with a colleague(s) you would like to send a file to.
2. Tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/0931bf1932daabce1ba5/64be2758ad7f08ab569dfd41bae6a526596862258be9c5a73f5d84a6d5f5a2f3.png)icon at the bottom of the screen.

![](/img/ringotel/original/c0aa4f6898b647e3779f/e9f9b9788867b3557cc08ad50184806dadc624e25ca87562ea1779e62886a0ed.png)

1. Select the type of message that you would like to send.

![](/img/ringotel/original/3a1e42a59dc3521b4b38/383eebfceedde9109f6f0af284b6542fb2d6f2852b4b8a6c380db97391dbf8e1.png)

## Sending voice messages

To send a voice message to your colleagues who are using the app:

1. Open a chat with a colleague(s) you would like to send a file to.
2. Tap on the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/63b816423febbcefd6ef/c3bef85b4b24029c6aa146e34738456c47718226171487c072ebbd9a45286a92.png)icon at the bottom of the screen.
3. The recording will start, and the appearance of the microphone icon will change.

![](/img/ringotel/original/53c289999bdba1675f12/a95e7278d9a59d10c3528a83c2f55b104fe109fc9152d8361561a0732b20ddc4.png)

1. After you’ve finished recording your message, tap again on the microphone icon to send you a message.

**To cancel the recording press and hold the microphone icon and slide it to the left.**
