## Copying a text message

To copy to a specific message in a chat:

1. Long-tap on a message that you’d like to copy.
2. In the top icon bar, press the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/34c83d882c2084f3c110/fbd3ec5a85dc703f830e001c359d8b3b3dbee901dd5b0072d52440e21fa07e7d.png)icon.

![](/img/ringotel/original/2041f9dbf6321fd06a8d/1c03ea85ec4a2184982f665fdb674d80701de5c1af748c2a6ac491dc27cda9a3.png)

## Replying to a message

To reply to a specific message in a chat:

1. Long-tap on a message that you’d like to reply to.
2. In the top icon bar, press the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/5b7d7c984cbc699c8b9e/b8c5a4d72fc4ef0d7dc1df91e98ecc557bf6725a41b2a400206e07d04141fdaa.png)icon.

![](/img/ringotel/original/2041f9dbf6321fd06a8d/1c03ea85ec4a2184982f665fdb674d80701de5c1af748c2a6ac491dc27cda9a3.png)

1. At the bottom of the screen, right above the Text Area where you type your messages, you’ll see the message that you’re replying to.

![](/img/ringotel/original/08e9949a59e6ea974231/f139cfa42972e4d215c53fb5ac89a47c5f28216dc943f9437f3eb03c57c5a898.png)

1. Type your reply in the text area at the bottom of the screen.
2. Press the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/38ebfc91dccd26a6cff6/c41538069d3c67cf954a3fb5d8b1149a8c357208824c15d350cadf9c0aea101e.png)icon to send your reply.

## Forwarding a message

To transfer a message to another colleague(s):

1. Long-tap on a message that you’d like to transfer.
2. In the top icon bar, press the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/519d904ec9f1688282a2/20bc807038852e74607a775f51a5bde559c5bca2b09b7f45f93011dbb8a40efa.png)icon.

![](/img/ringotel/original/2041f9dbf6321fd06a8d/1c03ea85ec4a2184982f665fdb674d80701de5c1af748c2a6ac491dc27cda9a3.png)

1. Select contacts or group chats to which you would like to forward a message.

![](/img/ringotel/original/346eeac128949d265e34/bfef963bed00c792de00740d15a94c927dc9691aff4e5fb1f2fb6a889c2e75f5.png)

## Share messages with other applications on your device

To share a message with other applications:

1. Long-tap on a message that you’d like to share.
2. Tap on the menu icon at the top right corner of the screen and choose ‘**Share**’ from the dropdown menu.

![](/img/ringotel/original/3a4028d438abc600000e/a4dcb383de1331779c5cda40493e58d83e90973c3d7b7aabb14f96ebe12dae34.png)

1. Select an application on your device you’d like to share a message with.

**You can choose this same application from the list to share a message with your colleagues who use this app.**
