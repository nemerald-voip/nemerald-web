After you’ve logged into the app, you’ll see a user interface as shown in a screenshot below:

![](/img/ringotel/original/de8dc2112ed8e6d6d597/327294adce9c9b5526d977c4936b66512108e7e7044de5cd108086f7710bb1c7.png)

The Navigation bar of the mobile app has the following tabs:

![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/1fc2c6808f194e127e53/8d64c7a558fa672db5f49d0f8ec8eefd3a5b7c95f5119f2e9b54d64231a64092.png)**Menu.**&#x48;ere you can find all the app settings, including your profile and presence status selection.

![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/66ac61ee986384589822/5e08fb300b5bf69116f73da41a62498b48ec959800d0eedfd8867300504b2f92.png)**Contacts.**&#x54;his is where you can find your colleagues and all your business contacts.

![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/ebfef0082d47259daf27/303daf2bacd63cb721e5cae49d1232122f65bdc3cf22aa393fdf86f349a8ea6e.png)**Chats.**&#x48;ere you can chat with your team, share files, photos, etc., and send SMS to your external contacts (only if the SMS feature is enabled for your account in the admin portal).

![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/25006e036b05ccf14a67/ad0641522953619d676cd3ac59ceb146b7bcb1a4f4f5d6687282919e94f53630.png)The**Calls**tab is where you can view the history of all the calls you’ve made.

![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/897e7b1ce59f34923691/f4cbc030b1254a1c7338da7f7c480ac280e9cb76ab14b1e3645aebd3a2f4b6dd.png)The**Keypad**tab is where you can dial a phone number to make a call.

![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/1fc04ec7d278977d80c6/5cf2affe3e4876763196bd1382b0cb3f09200ee70ab5fa4f92ee50cceddad819.png)**Speed dial.**&#x49;n this tab, you or your PBX administrator can add phone numbers, emergency contacts, and buttons to quickly access various PBX features.
