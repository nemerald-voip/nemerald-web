*Looking for an&#x20;*[PC](/docs/apps/desktop-working-with-call-recordings--ssv2vm6rzstcim2xd99giaun) *or&#x20;*[iOS](/docs/apps/ios-listening-sharing-recording--s99acnxuit527m1nl2j386zg) *version of this article?*

When you record a call using your mobile or desktop app, this call recording gets synchronized between all your devices. It means that you can listen to the recording of a call on your mobile device, even if it was recorded from your desktop application.

**To access a call recording from your mobile app**:

1. Open yourconversation historywith a contact.
2. Calls that have a voice recording will have an![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/c048edc7d5d0034abd8f/93d221051e6446cff673fd993608f4fe906157522ab4569d6f7ed81567701c2f.png)icon.

![](/img/ringotel/original/a89a3031db394f70dfd7/d3f8114e93718469236cad54f9ec1b5c58d0597c373a1c2029a5253c7cb591fa.png)

1. Tap on a ‘**View details**’ link of a call which recording you’d like to listen to.
2. On the **Call Details&#x20;**&#x70;anel, press the![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/d9d2c7847cb6c7d74b45/8bdfc157fba6e1ca623f6320bddc025b409eec63bfd8f6d88bd8c8838c459d3a.png)icon to playback a call recording.

![](/img/ringotel/original/2ea26dee921c133a49ac/9cfed62e048fd1d7dd3ec540ac7a28ae98cf8ce35e2b7a9c303cdd8cec0afd35.png)

**To share a call recording with other applications on your device**:

1. Open the details of a call with the recording you would like to share.
2. Tap on the menu icon![App icon](/img/ringotel/original/d404746dea3e7bbe0e32/c5dc0e365c7e0a5b48b11e90932007d994bbb6a8be3ded7bcd24e6c615397908.png).
3. Tap on the "Share recording".

![](/img/ringotel/original/e749bbd538f7badf5833/3f2d28e62654f6b720a6b172eea545ce5a059e887e03d32a80ccf897a8941a74.png)

1. Choose an application on your device where you’d like to send a recording.

**You can choose this same application from the list to share a recording with your colleagues who use this app.**

*\* Please check the availability of this feature with your PBX Administrator.*
