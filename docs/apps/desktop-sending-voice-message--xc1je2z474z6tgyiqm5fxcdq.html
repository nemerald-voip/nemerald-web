Sending a voice message

To send a voice message:

  1. Open the chat with a person you would like to send a voice message to.
  2. Click theicon at the right side of the Message Input Area.

  1. Click theicon at the center of the Message Recording Panel to start/pause the voice recording.

  1. Click on the chaticon to send your voice message.

To cancel the recording of a voice message, just click on theicon at the upper-right side of the voice recording panel.
