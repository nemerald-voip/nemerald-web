Creating a chat / Sending SMS

Availability of this feature depends on your service configuration. Contact support for details.

Looking for an Android or iOS version of this article?

To create a chat or send an SMS:

  1. Click on anicon at the right side of the tab name.
  2. Choose Create Chat from a drop-down menu.
  3. Select a User from the list to create a personal chat.

OR

  1. Enter a phone number in the search field and choose an external contact to create an SMS chat. *

* Please check the availability of this feature with your PBX Administrator.
