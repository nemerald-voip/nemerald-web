Making outgoing calls

There are several ways to initiate an outgoing call from the mobile app:

Making a call from a contact profile​

  1. Open the profile of a contact that you would like to call.
  2. Tap on theicon to make a call.

User’s profile example

External contact’s profile example

Making a call from a Keypad tab​

  1. Open the ‘Keypad’ tab by pressing theicon.
  2. Dial a phone number manually or tap on theicon to access your mobile device’s contacts list.

Making a call from a chat​

  1. Open the ‘Calls’ or ‘Chats’ tab.
  2. Open a chat with a contact that you would like to call.
  3. Press on aicon to make a call.
