Making outgoing calls

There are several ways to initiate an outgoing call from the mobile app:

Making a call from a contact profile​

  1. Open the profile of a contact/user that you would like to call.
  2. Tap on theicon to make a voice call or on theicon to make video call.

User’s profile example

External contact’s profile example

Making a call from a Calls tab​

  1. Open the ‘Calls’ tab

  1. Select a contact from your call history.
  2. Press on aicon to make a call.

Making a call from the Keypad​

  1. Open the Keypad tab.
  2. Enter the phone number you'd like to call.

  1. Press on abutton to make a call.
